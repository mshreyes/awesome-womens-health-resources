# Awesome Women's Health Resources [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

Openly accessible, reputable resources that support the biology and health of women: clinical guidance, datasets, open-access research, and open-source software and apps. This is not a femtech directory. Everything here is free to read or has a free access tier, and is linked to an institutional, peer-reviewed or open-source origin.

**Interactive view:** see [awesome-womens-health-resources](https://mshreyes.github.io/awesome-womens-health-resources/).

> This list is for education and research. It is not medical advice. For personal health concerns, talk to a qualified clinician.

## Trusted Health Information

- [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/) - US National Library of Medicine consumer health encyclopaedia, with women's health topics, drug information and lab-test explainers; also in Spanish.
- [Office on Women's Health (HHS)](https://womenshealth.gov/) - US federal portal covering the life course: periods, pregnancy, menopause, mental health, violence and screening.
- [NHS Health A-Z](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/) - UK National Health Service condition pages with symptoms, treatment and when to seek care.
- [ACOG Patient Education](https://www.acog.org/womens-health) - American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists FAQs and patient guides, written by clinicians.
- [Cochrane Library](https://www.cochranelibrary.com/) - Systematic reviews of interventions. Abstracts and plain-language summaries are free; includes a large pregnancy and childbirth collection.
- [NICE Guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance) - UK evidence-based guidelines, including menopause, endometriosis, fertility, antenatal and postnatal care.
- [WHO Reproductive Health Library](https://srhr.org/rhl/) - WHO-supported evidence summaries and practical guidance on sexual and reproductive health.

## Contraception and Fertility

- [Family Planning: A Global Handbook for Providers](https://fphandbook.org/) - WHO / Johns Hopkins CCP handbook on contraceptive methods, eligibility and counselling.
- [CDC Contraception](https://www.cdc.gov/contraception/) - CDC contraception pages, including the US Medical Eligibility Criteria and Selected Practice Recommendations.
- [ESHRE Guidelines](https://www.eshre.eu/Guidelines-and-Legal) - European Society of Human Reproduction and Embryology guidelines on infertility, endometriosis, recurrent pregnancy loss and more.
- [Human Fertility Database](https://www.humanfertility.org/) - Harmonised fertility statistics by country (Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research and Vienna Institute of Demography). Free registration. *(free registration)*

## Pregnancy, Lactation and Maternal Health

- [MotherToBaby](https://mothertobaby.org/) - Nonprofit fact sheets on medication, vaccine and exposure safety in pregnancy and breastfeeding.
- [LactMed](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501922/) - NLM database of drugs and their levels in breast milk and effects on nursing infants; peer-reviewed and referenced.
- [WHO Maternal Health](https://www.who.int/health-topics/maternal-health) - WHO guidance on antenatal, intrapartum and postnatal care and maternal mortality.
- [Postpartum Support International](https://postpartum.net/) - Nonprofit providing information and peer support for perinatal mood and anxiety disorders.
- [Open Systems Pharmacology: Pregnancy Models](https://github.com/Open-Systems-Pharmacology/Pregnancy-Models) - Physiologically based pharmacokinetic models for pregnant women, for modelling drug exposure in pregnancy.
- [AllofUs_Pregnancy_Episodes](https://github.com/DreisbachLab/AllofUs_Pregnancy_Episodes) - Scripts for identifying pregnancy episodes in the All of Us Research Program data (MIT licence).

## Menstrual Health and Gynaecological Conditions

- [PCOS International Guideline](https://www.monash.edu/medicine/mchri/pcos) - Evidence-based international guideline for the assessment and management of polycystic ovary syndrome, with translations and clinician and patient summaries.
- [Endometriosis UK](https://www.endometriosis-uk.org/) - Charity with patient information on endometriosis and adenomyosis.
- [Endometriosis-Adenomyosis-Resources](https://github.com/bloo-berries/Endometriosis-Adenomyosis-Resources) - Community-curated CC0 list of endometriosis and adenomyosis resources. Check the primary sources it points to.
- [AtlasEndometriosis](https://github.com/lawrenson-lab/AtlasEndometriosis) - Code for a single-cell transcriptomic analysis of endometriosis, endometriomas and eutopic endometrium (no licence file).

## Menopause and Bone Health

- [The Menopause Society](https://menopause.org/) - Professional society with patient-facing information on menopause and its management.
- [British Menopause Society](https://thebms.org.uk/) - UK clinical society with patient fact sheets and evidence-based consensus statements.
- [SWAN: Study of Women's Health Across the Nation](https://www.swanstudy.org/) - Longitudinal US cohort of the menopausal transition; public-use data are distributed through ICPSR. *(free registration)*
- [FRAX](https://frax.shef.ac.uk/) - WHO-linked fracture risk calculator developed at the University of Sheffield.
- [Bone Health and Osteoporosis Foundation](https://www.bonehealthandosteoporosis.org/) - US nonprofit offering osteoporosis prevention and treatment information.

## Cancer and Screening

- [National Cancer Institute](https://www.cancer.gov/) - Evidence-reviewed PDQ summaries on breast, cervical, ovarian and endometrial cancer, plus screening information.
- [Genomic Data Commons](https://portal.gdc.cancer.gov/) - TCGA and related cohorts including breast, ovarian, cervical and uterine cancers. Open-access tier plus controlled data.
- [cBioPortal](https://www.cbioportal.org/) - Interactive exploration of cancer genomics; has an R client (cBioPortalData) and API.
- [BRCA Exchange](https://brcaexchange.org/) - Global Alliance for Genomics and Health aggregation of BRCA1 and BRCA2 variant interpretations.
- [The Cancer Imaging Archive](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/) - Public medical imaging collections, including mammography datasets.
- [Mirai](https://github.com/yala/Mirai) (Python) - Python code for a mammography-based breast cancer risk model (MIT licence). Research code, not a clinical tool.
- [consensusOV](https://github.com/bhklab/consensusOV) (R) - R/Bioconductor package implementing four subtype classifiers for high-grade serous ovarian cancer.

## Sex and Gender in Research

- [Biology of Sex Differences](https://bsd.biomedcentral.com/) - Open-access journal on sex differences in physiology and disease.
- [SAGER Guidelines](https://ease.org.uk/communities/gender-policy-committee/the-sager-guidelines/) - European Association of Science Editors guidelines for reporting sex and gender in study design, analysis and publication.
- [NIH Office of Research on Women's Health](https://orwh.od.nih.gov/) - NIH office guiding research on women's health and the inclusion of women and sex as a biological variable.
- [GTEx Portal](https://gtexportal.org/) - Tissue gene expression across donors, including sex-biased expression and female reproductive tissues.
- [Human Protein Atlas](https://www.proteinatlas.org/) - Protein and RNA expression across human tissues, including ovary, endometrium, breast and placenta.

## Datasets and Statistics

- [DHS Program](https://dhsprogram.com/) - Nationally representative household surveys in 90+ countries on fertility, family planning, maternal and child health. Free registration. *(free registration)*
- [IPUMS DHS](https://www.idhsdata.org/) - Harmonised DHS microdata across countries and survey waves. Free registration. *(free registration)*
- [PMA Data](https://www.pmadata.org/) - Performance Monitoring for Action: open survey data on family planning, maternal health and reproductive health in several countries.
- [UNICEF MICS](https://mics.unicef.org/) - Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys with women's health, fertility and maternal indicators. *(free registration)*
- [WHO Global Health Observatory](https://www.who.int/data/gho) - WHO indicator data with API and bulk downloads, including maternal and reproductive health.
- [World Bank Gender Data Portal](https://genderdata.worldbank.org/) - Sex-disaggregated indicators on health, education and economic participation.
- [Global Burden of Disease Results](https://vizhub.healthdata.org/gbd-results/) - IHME estimates of disease burden by sex, age and location. Free for non-commercial use with registration. *(free registration)*
- [CDC National Survey of Family Growth](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nsfg/) - US survey of pregnancy, contraception, infertility and sexual health, with public-use files.
- [NHANES](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/) - US nutrition and health examination survey; analysable by sex and reproductive status.
- [All of Us Research Program](https://www.researchallofus.org/) - Large US cohort with EHR, survey and genomic data. Free registered-access tier for approved researchers. *(approval or controlled access)*
- [Women's Health Initiative (BioLINCC)](https://biolincc.nhlbi.nih.gov/) - WHI trial and observational data on postmenopausal women, available on request through NHLBI BioLINCC. *(approval or controlled access)*

## Open-Source Cycle Trackers

- [drip.](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.drip/) - Offline, non-commercial cycle and fertility tracker by the Bloody Health collective (GPL-3.0). On F-Droid and GitHub (bloodyhealth/drip). Check release activity before relying on it.
- [Mensinator](https://github.com/EmmaTellblom/Mensinator) - Privacy-focused Android period tracker (MIT); listed on F-Droid.
- [Menstrudel](https://github.com/J-shw/Menstrudel) - Free, offline, open-source period tracker (GPL-3.0).
- [Ovumcy](https://github.com/ovumcy/ovumcy-web) - Self-hosted, privacy-first menstrual cycle tracker, Docker-ready (AGPL-3.0).
- [peri](https://github.com/IraSoro/peri) - Open-source period tracker app (MIT).
- [Bluemoon](https://search.f-droid.org/?q=Bluemoon) - Privacy-friendly menstruation tracker on F-Droid (GPL-3.0-or-later). Search link used; confirm the package page.
- [Periodical](https://search.f-droid.org/?q=Periodical) - Menstrual calendar with fertile-day estimates by the Knaus-Ogino calendar method, on F-Droid (GPL-3.0-or-later). The method is less reliable than modern fertility-awareness methods; treat as a calendar, not contraception.
- [log28](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.log28/) - No-frills period tracker on F-Droid (LGPL-3.0). Latest F-Droid release is from 2020; check maintenance first.
- [fertility-test-analyzer](https://github.com/lrq3000/fertility-test-analyzer) - Android app for quantifying ovulation and pregnancy test strips (Apache-2.0).
- [Mooneva-Cycle-Private-Period-Tracker](https://github.com/aradar46/Mooneva-Cycle-Private-Period-Tracker) - Period tracking app - Free, offline, private, and open source. Android and IOS- no account, no cloud, no ads. GPL-3.0, on-device encryption, zero internet permission. symptom & mood tracking, PIN lock, discrete mode.

## Evaluating Sources

Fake or low-quality medical sites are common. Before adding or relying on a resource, check that it:

- is run by an identifiable institution (government, university, hospital, professional society, established nonprofit) with a named editorial or review process;
- cites primary literature or guidelines and shows when the page was last reviewed;
- does not sell supplements, "detox" kits or miracle cures, or promise to cure chronic conditions;
- does not push alarmist or "secret the doctors hide" claims, and avoids paywalled "diagnosis" quizzes that collect personal data;
- states how health data is handled, if it is an app (prefer offline or self-hosted, open-source code).

For research use, prefer primary sources (guidelines, systematic reviews, registered datasets) over summaries.

---

## Inclusion Criteria

1. Directly relevant to women's biology or health, including sex-specific or sex-disaggregated data.
2. Freely accessible. Free-registration or approval-gated data is allowed and labelled.
3. From a reputable institution, peer-reviewed venue, or open-source project with a stated licence and recent activity.
4. No commercial femtech products, affiliate links or ad-driven content sites.
5. Community-curated lists are included only when they link to primary sources, and are flagged.

GitHub projects were checked for licence and recent activity when added. Research code is not a clinical tool.
